# Riverbend Medical Practice — Parking and Accessibility

SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DATA — NOT A REAL GP PRACTICE

## Parking

Riverbend Medical Practice has a small car park for patients and visitors
located at the rear of the building (access from Station Road).

### Parking Charges

- First 30 minutes: Free
- Up to 2 hours: £2.00
- Over 2 hours: £5.00

### Disabled Parking

Four designated disabled parking bays are available at the front of the
building, closest to the main entrance. A valid Blue Badge must be displayed.

## Accessibility

### Building Access

- The main entrance has level access with automatic doors
- Wheelchair access is available throughout the ground floor
- An accessible toilet is located near the waiting area
- A hearing loop is available at the reception desk

### Public Transport

The practice is served by the following bus routes:
- Route 12: City Centre to Riverbend (stops outside the practice)
- Route 15: Hospital to University (stops on High Street, 2-minute walk)
- Route 23: Suburbs to City Centre (stops on High Street, 5-minute walk)

The nearest train station is Bristol Temple Meads, approximately 15 minutes by
taxi or 25 minutes by bus.

## Home Visits

Home visits are available for housebound patients. Please contact the practice
on 0117 555 0123 to request a home visit assessment.
