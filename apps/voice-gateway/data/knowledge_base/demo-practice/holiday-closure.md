## During Closures

For urgent medical needs when the practice is closed:
- Call NHS 111 for urgent but non-life-threatening medical advice
- Call 999 in a medical emergency
- Visit your local pharmacy for minor health concerns

## Repeat Prescriptions During Closures

If you need a repeat prescription during a closure period, please request it at
least 3 working days before the closure to ensure continuity of your medication.
