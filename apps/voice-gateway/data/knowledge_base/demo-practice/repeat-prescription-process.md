# Riverbend Medical Practice — Repeat Prescription Process

SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DATA — NOT A REAL GP PRACTICE

## How to Request a Repeat Prescription

Patients registered with Riverbend Medical Practice can request repeat
prescriptions through the following methods:

### Online

Use the NHS App or our online prescription request form at
www.riverbendpractice.nhs.uk/prescriptions. Requests are reviewed by our
clinical team and usually processed within 3 working days.

### Telephone

Call our prescription line on 0117 555 0125 during practice hours (Monday to
Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM). Please have your NHS number ready.

### In Person

Drop a written request at the reception desk at Riverbend Medical Practice
during opening hours.

### By Post

Send a written request to:
Riverbend Medical Practice  
123 High Street  
Bristol  
BS1 4AB

## What Information to Provide

When requesting a repeat prescription, please provide:

- Your full name and date of birth
- Your NHS number (if known)
- The name of the medication
- The strength and dosage
- Your contact telephone number

## What Happens Next

1. Your request is reviewed by our clinical team (usually a GP)
2. If approved, the prescription is sent electronically to your chosen pharmacy
3. You can collect your medication from the pharmacy
4. If the clinical team needs more information, we will contact you

## Processing Time

Repeat prescription requests are usually processed within 3 working days.
Please allow at least 48 hours before contacting us about the status of your
request.

## Important Notes

- We cannot prescribe new medications through the repeat prescription service.
  New medications require a GP appointment.
- We cannot process requests for controlled drugs through this service.
- Please do not request prescriptions more than 7 days in advance of when you
  need them.
