# Riverbend Medical Practice — Appointment Request Process

SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DATA — NOT A REAL GP PRACTICE

## How to Request an Appointment

Patients can request an appointment through the following methods:

### Online

Use our online appointment request form at www.riverbendpractice.nhs.uk/book.
Forms are reviewed during practice hours and you will receive a response within
2 working days.

### Telephone

Call our appointment line on 0117 555 0124 during practice hours (Monday to
Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM).

### In Person

Visit the reception desk at Riverbend Medical Practice during opening hours.

## What Information to Provide

When requesting an appointment, please have the following ready:

- Your full name and date of birth
- Your NHS number (if known)
- Your contact telephone number
- The reason for your appointment (briefly)
- Any preferred date or time

## What Happens Next

1. Your request is reviewed by our reception team
2. We match your request with an available clinician
3. You receive confirmation by telephone or SMS
4. If we cannot offer your preferred time, we will suggest alternatives

## Appointment Types

- Routine GP appointment (10 minutes)
- Longer GP appointment (20 minutes) — for multiple health concerns
- Nurse appointment (15 minutes)
- Healthcare assistant appointment (10 minutes)
- Telephone consultation (10 minutes)

## Cancellation

If you cannot attend your appointment, please let us know as soon as possible
so we can offer the slot to another patient. You can cancel by calling
0117 555 0124 or using the online form.
